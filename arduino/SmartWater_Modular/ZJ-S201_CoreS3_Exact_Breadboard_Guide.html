Use These Exact Holes on Your Breadboard

Upper A–E strip, columns 10–20 • left power-rail section only • M5Stack CoreS3 GPIO2

CoreS3 Port A wires

1 RED (5V) → red + rail near column 8
2 BLACK (GND) → blue − rail near column 8
7 YELLOW (G2/GPIO2) → B15
WHITE (G1) → leave disconnected and capped

The rail hole number is not electrically important; staying on the same left rail segment is.

ZJ-S201 sensor wires

3 RED (5V) → same red + rail near column 18
4 BLACK (GND) → same blue − rail near column 18
5 YELLOW (OUT) → B10
Extra gray/white → leave disconnected and capped

Power must be OFF while moving wires.

Resistors and shared node

6 — 10 kΩ: A10 → A15
7 — Core GPIO2 yellow: B15
8 — 20 kΩ: D15 → D20
9 — black jumper: E20 → top blue GND rail
A15, B15, C15, D15 and E15 are one connected strip.

Confirm resistor values with a multimeter before powering.
